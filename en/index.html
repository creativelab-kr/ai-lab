Principles

Three rules for everything here

Facts and guesses are kept apart

What we verified and what we assume are labelled separately.

Every number has a date and a source

When it was measured and where it can be checked. Other people's numbers are marked as theirs.

Mistakes stay on the record

Wrong forecasts and false "done" reports are corrected in the open, with dates.
